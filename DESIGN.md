# Portfolio design

The homepage opens with Joydip’s introduction and his own portrait. A curved field of muted sage frames the image. Engineering projects get full-width case-study rows, followed by a compact newspaper-style blog and a smaller photography feature.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Sage field | Muted Sage Grey, #A5A79B |
| Warm accent | Warm Cream Beige, #E8D2B5 |
| Brand and primary actions | Deep Burgundy, #590907 |
| Light reading canvas | #F5EFE5 |
| Dark reading canvas | #1D231F |

Fraunces carries display headlines, DM Sans supports navigation and interface text, and article prose uses Georgia in a narrow column. The blog uses a double rule, lead photography or project art, and readable headline, summary, and metadata. Keep motion minimal and use layout and visible focus to communicate state.
